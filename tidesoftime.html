This page of our Austronesian Adventures is dedicated to the history and expansion of the Austronesian peoples. The Austronesians are a group of people who speak Austronesian languages and are spread across a vast area from Madagascar to Easter Island, and from Taiwan to New Zealand. They are known for their seafaring skills and have a rich cultural heritage that includes unique art, music, and traditions. The Austronesians are believed to have originated in Taiwan and began their expansion around 3000 BCE. They traveled across the Pacific and Indian Oceans, settling in various islands and regions along the way.

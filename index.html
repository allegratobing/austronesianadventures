What is "Austronesia"?

Austronesia is a region that encompasses the islands of Southeast Asia, the Pacific Ocean, and parts of Madagascar. It is home to a diverse range of cultures, languages, and traditions that have developed over thousands of years. The Austronesian peoples are known for their seafaring skills, which allowed them to explore and settle across vast distances. This website aims to provide a comprehensive overview of Austronesian history, culture, and migration patterns.
